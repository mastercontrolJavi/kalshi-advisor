## Method and limits

- Walk-forward: 6 expanding folds by round time; each model sees only rounds that closed before its test block, with the last 20% of its window held out for early stopping and Platt calibration.
- The journal covers about 1,000 rounds (Oct 7 → now) and has no data from 4 to 10 AM PT; the 1-minute archive covers 10,878 rounds but only minute-end quotes, so it cannot test maker fills or the final minute.
- Taker entries pay the ask one second after the decision. Maker fills require the market to trade through the limit; a real queue could fill sooner (the 'touch' row) but then adverse selection is larger, not smaller.
- 1 contract per signal keeps depth out of it. At the sizes you traded (often 1,000+ contracts) you would also walk the book.
- Settlement uses Kalshi's [close − 60 s, close) BRTI average, which reproduces Kalshi's value on 983/983 checked rounds.
