## Collected evidence

Read-only inspection of `data/advisor.db` found 319 settled outcomes (299 with paired proxy/index errors), 1,475 opening/live calls, and 3,600 ticks in the latest hour with no missing proxy or model values. There were no logged error events in that hour. Counts are a point-in-time snapshot, not permanent totals.

The latest recorded fit used 309 settled markets. Its cross-validated Brier score was 0.13818 versus 0.13870 for the default blend; the improvement was smaller than the engine's 0.002 adoption margin, so the default blend remains active. No captured calls had an explicitly rejected midpoint at the time of inspection; preserving null fixes a latent training inconsistency for future calls.

The saved backtest covered 8,988 rounds. The quant entry rule averaged −0.65¢ per contract after fees over 2,626 trades, with a range of approximately −2.16¢ to +0.93¢. The final-minute sample had only 72 trades and a wide range. These results support retaining the existing adoption gates and entry settings, rather than increasing signal frequency based on this UI update.

## Functional changes and limits

- Quant blending rejects invalid, stale, future-dated, crossed, missing, or wide books. Training preserves the exact stored midpoint feature, including explicit null, with a fallback for legacy calls. Final-minute explanations use the forecast that actually generated the call.
- Chart history merges with ticks received during requests; data is retained by time rather than a 4 Hz sample-count limit. Missing intervals do not create fabricated candle movement. The first history load gets a useful range, later refreshes preserve zoom and the viewed time range, and Latest restores the recent view.
- History and sharps requests have timeouts, avoid overlapping requests, and cancel on unmount. A failed sharps fetch clears the previous response.
- Entry and exit advice are suppressed when state is stale, and an unavailable positions feed is distinguished from an empty account.

Visual review and native keyboard interactions do not establish full accessibility compliance. The financial checks here review the app's own saved evidence; they do not establish a profitable strategy. The app remains an advisor with its existing disabled order executor.
